# Commercial Studio Lease — Controllable vs. Uncontrollable CAM Cap

## Constraints
Constraints: Total monthly occupancy cost (Base Rent + NNN CAM charges) must not exceed $4,300/month in Year 1 OR Year 2 under stated escalation terms. The space fits our studio perfectly and the broker recommends signing before another tenant takes it, so I am leaning toward signing unless the numbers can exceed $4,300/month in Year 2.

## Packet
Lease Abstract — Suite 204, Alder Creek Flex Park (2,000 sq ft).
1. Base Rent: $3,200/month in Year 1, escalating 3.0% annually in Year 2.
2. Operating Expenses (Clause 9 — NNN CAM): Tenant pays pro-rata share of building operating expenses, currently $950/month. Clause 9 caps annual increases in Controllable Operating Expenses at 3.0% per year.
3. CAM Breakdown (Exhibit B): Controllable expenses (janitorial, landscaping, management fee) represent $330/month of current CAM. Uncontrollable expenses—defined in Clause 9 as property taxes, building insurance, and common utilities—represent $620/month and are excluded from the 3% cap, passed through at actual cost. County reassessment notice in packet projects Year-2 property taxes and insurance rising by $185/month for this suite.
4. Parking & Deposit: Six reserved stalls included; two-month security deposit.
